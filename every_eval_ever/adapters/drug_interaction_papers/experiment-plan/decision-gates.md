# Decision Gates

| Gate ID | Opens after | Decision question | Proceed if | Revise if | Stop if | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G1 | B1 | Is the selected source-cell universe frozen, complete, version-consistent, and independently checkable? | All enabled bundles, expected counts, foreign keys, and anchor cells pass; every negative-control mutation fails; and a materially separate reviewer records independent source-cell verification. | Correct locators, counts, manifests, or verification status and rerun without changing acceptance thresholds post hoc. | Any source remains mixed-version, unlocatable, unresolved, or outcome-selected. | Adapter author plus source verifier |
| G2 | B2 | Does the mapping preserve the paper-native task, protocol, metric, identity, provenance, and licensing semantics? | Zero invariant violations, unsupported global identities, protocol collisions, metric-semantic collisions, or leakage findings. | Narrow or namespace an underspecified mapping and rerun. | A selected result cannot be represented without making an unsupported semantic claim. | Adapter author plus adversarial reviewer |
| G3 | B3 | Does the full converter produce exactly complete, schema-valid, logically deterministic output? | Counts match manifests, all records validate, logical IDs are unique, routing is correct, and two runs are semantically identical. | Fix source/mapping/schema/publication defect and regenerate from clean state. | Any unexplained omission, duplicate, schema failure, or nondeterministic semantic field remains. | Adapter author |
| G4 | B4 | Does every predefined failure mode fail closed and preserve prior output? | All mutations exit nonzero, publish nothing partial, preserve prior digests, and emit complete failure accounting. | Correct error boundary or atomic publication and rerun the full matrix. | Any failure exits cleanly, silently skips selected data, or changes prior output. | Adapter author plus reviewer |
| G5 | B5 | Is the generated corpus ready to support a later upstream adapter PR and separate datastore PR? | Zero logical/path collisions, missing provenance fields, leakage hits, stale files, or release-audit failures; exact code/source snapshots recorded; G1 independently passes. | Correct identities, routing, metadata, or scope and regenerate. | Source verification remains single-pass without disclosure, LLMDDI is mixed-version, or any release-blocking finding remains. | Upstream PR reviewer / maintainer |
