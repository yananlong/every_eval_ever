N. Provenance resampling on EEE: how much ECI moves with the source of each score

Section N.1 of the EEE mirror replaced Epoch's score by one EEE rule in each covered cell. This exploratory section asks a different question: if the score in each covered cell is drawn from a different evaluation source, how far does ECI move? Epoch's 688 covered cells, the 2,879-cell design and Epoch's priors stay as they are, and only the score in a covered cell changes. Everything holds under Epoch's model, and the design goes beyond the registered plan.

The answer depends on where a model sits. At the frontier the provenance SD of the posterior-mean ECI is at most 0.38 points when every source is eligible (0.13 for independent sources, 0.11 for own-run sources), which is 0.05 of a 90% interval width at most and 1.5 times the 0.25 seed SD for the widest case. The top 10 is the same set in every refit. The largest spread is in weak 2023 models, up to 4.27 points (Cerebras-GPT-13B), which have 0 multi-source cells of their own, so it comes from re-estimated benchmark difficulties.

N.1 Design

Each refit gives every one of the 688 covered cells (cells with at least one EEE source in the crosswalk, 3rd audit sample accepted as recorded in runs/EEE/PROGRESS.md) a score drawn uniformly and independently from a pool. In variant R the pool is Epoch's value plus the score of each EEE source (per source the highest score in the cell). In variant I the pool holds independent sources only, and in variant O own-run sources only. Cells with no source in the pool keep Epoch's value, and a cell with one source takes it, so only cells with two or more sources vary between replicates. Each replicate has its own recorded RNG seed. Every refit uses fit seed 12345, Epoch's unmodified fit and priors, 4,000 draws per chain, and the retry rules of the benchmark-selection refits.

The Lab-by-benchmark check (T5) sits on the edge of the 0.01 level. Its observed statistic ranges from 3.851 to 4.868 across refits, between the EEE-data fit and the Epoch-data fit and above both at times. The adjusted p moves in steps of about 0.005, and the typical refit gives 0.0104, just above the level. In variant R, 8 of 50 refits reject; in variants I and O, 1 and 0. This indicates that T5's non-rejection on the EEE input was a near call that depends on which sources are drawn. T1 and T2 reject in every refit and T3, T4, T6 and T7 in none. The full cost of per-refit replicate sets, about 200 fits each, was not paid.

N.6 What drives the spread

A model's spread is not related to how many of its own cells have several sources (Spearman -0.12 in R); it is related to how much the difficulties of its benchmarks are re-estimated (0.69). Benchmarks with the largest difficulty SD across replicates: TriviaQA (0.500, 5 multi-source cells), LAMBADA (0.292, 2 multi-source cells), PIQA (0.226, 8 multi-source cells), OSWorld 2.0 (0.217, 3 multi-source cells), VPCT (0.194, 3 multi-source cells), HellaSwag (0.186, 5 multi-source cells). Models with no multi-source cell have a median SD of 1.04, those with some 0.28. The same pattern as in the EEE mirror: old benchmarks near the low end of the scale are re-estimated, and weak models that sit on them move with them.

N.7 Limits

Replicates are draws from the crosswalk as it stands, with the cell set fixed to Epoch's. The crosswalk was built on EEE manifest 959f0e11; a newer revision would add about 2 cells and was not rebuilt. Uniform draws weight Epoch's value more where a same-upstream source copies it. Sampler noise is inside every SD because the fit seed is shared. These results suggest where source choice matters, and say nothing about the 2,191 cells without an EEE source or the 24 benchmarks with none.
