N. Mirror on EEE data: the verdicts repeat, and the low end of the scale moves

Every Eval Ever (EEE) stores published evaluation results from many sources in one schema. This section rebuilds Epoch's fit input from EEE for the same 2,879 (model, benchmark) cells and runs the whole pipeline of sections 9 to 15 on it with the same scripts, seeds, caps and rules. Everything here holds under Epoch's model.

EEE covers 688 of the 2,879 cells, and 366 of them differ from Epoch's value. The other cells keep Epoch's score, so this is a partial mirror: about one cell in eight changes. On that matrix every registered verdict comes out as it did on Epoch's data. C2 is falsified, C1 is falsified, intervals are calibrated when the model is true, and held-out coverage is on target. Two things differ. The Lab-by-benchmark check (T5) no longer rejects at 0.01 family-wise, and the ECI scores of the weakest models rise by up to 13.5 points although none of their own scores changed.

N.1 Data, crosswalk and audit

The data are the latest EEE flat manifest, core hash 959f0e11, created 2026-11-20 with 127,491 aggregate records in 129 collections, read from the Hugging Face dataset evaleval/EEE_datastore at revision 5bce4753. The plan had pinned manifest dd7efcff of 2026-11-13. The latest one adds 4,630 records and removes none. All 127,491 files matched their manifest hashes.

The crosswalk follows the plan's method M9. A hand-made benchmark map keeps an EEE result only when benchmark, version, split and metric match Epoch's column, which leaves 101 entries for 33 of the 57 ECI benchmarks. A model map ties 1,356 EEE model ids to 220 of the 256 fitted model groups. Scores are put on Epoch's scale with each benchmark's random baseline and ceiling. Each pair is classed as independent when the EEE source ran the benchmark itself, same-upstream when it copies the measurement Epoch used, and upstream-unknown otherwise. For the 312 same-upstream pairs the median absolute difference from Epoch's value is 0.0000 against a limit of 0.02.

The registered audit draws 50 pairs and allows at most 2 errors. The first sample failed with 8 errors, all in the source class, because the benchmark's home page had been read as the reference of the score. After the fix a second sample had 0 errors. A later check of the two anchors then found an error the sample had missed: the undated id claude-3.5-sonnet had been matched to the June 2024 model and carried an October 2024 score into the low anchor. Undated ids that fit two dated groups are now left unmapped, the maps were rebuilt, and a third sample had 0 errors in 50 (95% upper bound 5.8%). The plan allows one re-audit, so the third sample is a deviation, and the audit is a self-check by the analyst who built the maps.

In the fit input a cell with an EEE score takes it, with independent sources preferred, then same-upstream, then upstream-unknown, and the highest score across sources within the class, as in Epoch's best-of rule.

Coverage is uneven. GPQA diamond holds 149 of the 280 independent cells, and Terminal Bench, BBH, SimpleQA Verified, MATH level 5 and OpenBookQA most of the rest. ARC-AGI, ARC-AGI-2, ProofBench and FrontierCode are covered almost fully, but by copies of the leaderboards Epoch itself cites, so they change almost nothing. Twenty-four benchmarks have no EEE counterpart at all, among them OTIS Mock AIME, DTBench, LMCA and WeirdML. Both anchors keep all their cells. Claude 3.5 Sonnet has 2 of 12 from EEE and GPT-5 has 6 of 28.

An EEE-only matrix was not fitted. Only 48 models would meet Epoch's inclusion rule and Claude 3.5 Sonnet is not among them, which fails the plan's size condition of 15 benchmarks and 100 models.

T1 and T2 reject at 0.01 family-wise on the EEE matrix, each beyond all 192 model-true replicates. T5 does not: its observed value falls from 4.45 to 3.86 and its adjusted p is 0.016. The largest cell on Epoch's data, Anthropic on GPQA diamond with t = -4.45, is gone, and GPQA diamond is the benchmark where EEE replaces most cells with scores from other evaluators. The OpenAI offsets on the FrontierMath tiers remain the largest cells. This suggests that part of the Lab-by-benchmark signal on Epoch's data belongs to Epoch's own GPQA runs. T3 moves toward rejection (adjusted p 0.09 against 0.81) without reaching it.

Localisation gives 17 benchmarks, above the limit of 10, with 15 in common with the 16 found before. Deleting them leaves T1 and T2 beyond every model-true replicate. Under the registered rule C2 is falsified on this matrix as well. Held-out coverage is 0.900 and no benchmark is flagged.

Prior sensitivity gives the same verdict. P8 and P9 move 58 and 61 of 254 models by more than a quarter of their interval width, against a limit of 12, and no frontier model. C1 is falsified. The hyperprior and noise priors move nothing and the top 10 holds under every prior. The shifts under P8 and P9 are smaller in points (largest 19.0 against 28.8) because the weakest models already sit higher on the EEE matrix.

The sampler behaved differently. Every prior from P1 to P10 resolved on its first attempt in about six minutes, including P3, which stayed unresolved on Epoch's data after two attempts of two hours, and P9 and P10, which had needed a third attempt. The Jeffreys prior J2 went the other way and hit the two-hour cap, so it is unresolved here. Posterior SBC was not run and J1 was not fitted, as before.

N.4 What this mirror can and cannot show

The mirror answers a narrow question: whether the findings of this page depend on Epoch's own collection of scores for the cells where a second collection exists. For the 366 changed cells they do not, apart from T5. It cannot speak for the 2,191 cells without an EEE score, and 210 of the covered cells are copies of Epoch's sources. A full test of data dependence would need independent scores on the benchmarks Epoch runs itself, which EEE holds for GPQA diamond and for little else.

Three choices could be made differently: the highest score across sources within a class, the use of upstream-unknown scores, and the exclusion of undated model ids. None was varied here. Deviations are listed in runs/deviations_local.md and runs/EEE/SUMMARY.md.
