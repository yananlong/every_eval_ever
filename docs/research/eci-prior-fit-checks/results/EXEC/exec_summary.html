Executive summary

The Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI) places AI models on one scale by fitting a one-dimensional Beta item-response model to 2,879 scores from 256 models on 57 benchmarks, with Claude 3.5 Sonnet anchored at 130 and GPT-5 at 150. Under Epoch's model refitted to Epoch's data, the computation is sound: 0 divergences, a maximum R-hat of 1.004, 90% interval coverage of 0.882 in simulation (0.903 at the frontier). The single-factor structure fails. On the real data the Curve-shape (T1), Residual-correlation (T2) and Lab-by-benchmark (T5) checks reject at adjusted p 0.0053, beyond all 187 model-true replicates, and the misfit spans 16 benchmarks. With those deleted, T1 and T2 still reject (p 0.0055), so the Model-adequacy claim (C2) is falsified. How far this misfit moves ECI scores has not been measured (W6 is pending), and nothing here shows a published score or ranking to be wrong.

The prior matters at the low end. Two proper capability priors (P8, P9) move 59 and 61 of 254 models by more than a quarter of their interval width, against a limit of 12, so the Prior-robustness claim (C1) is falsified. The models below ECI 100 rise by up to about 29 points under P9, while the frontier rises by at most 1.7 points and the top-10 set is unchanged.

Rankings are better read as tiers. Only first place is clear, with a lead of 3.64 points, while ranks 2 to 9 lie within 3.29 points, most adjacent pairs have ordering probabilities of 0.51 to 0.76, and the top-10 set appears in 64.7% of posterior draws against 0.75% for its exact order. Pairwise probabilities are probably overconfident given correlated residuals, an inference we have not tested. Gaps also depend on the benchmark set. Removing OTIS Mock AIME stretches the frontier by 2.5 points and shifts every frontier model by more than a quarter of its interval width, and random 20% drops move the low end as much as the capability priors do (section 15.5). Changing the anchor values only rescales ECI. Nine other anchor pairs leave every ranking and pairwise probability unchanged, and with GPT-4o mini as the low anchor P9 also fails at the frontier (section 15.4).
