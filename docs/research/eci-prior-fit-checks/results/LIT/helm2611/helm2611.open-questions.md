# helm2611 open questions

## Contradictions

- **Abstract vs body, "outperform baselines ... across two large benchmark suites".** Table 1 has DKPS equal to or within 0.002 of PKPS at HELM m = 8 (0.111 vs 0.109) and the sample score ahead of PKPS at HELM m = 8 (0.092 vs 0.109); only the ensemble is best there. HELM full-cohort completion gain is not significant (9 of 18 tasks). The abstract says "PKPS-based methods", which the ensemble satisfies, but PKPS alone does not dominate (tex:138-143, tex:585-587).
- **"up to 8x" and "up to 28%".** Both are maxima attained on the EEE suite; the HELM counterparts are 4x and about 5% (tex:110-112, tex:549, tex:585-587, tex:612-613).
- **Sensitivity wording.** Body: MAE moves "by less than 0.02" over d = 4 to 24 (tex:619-621). Appendix: sweep is d in {4, 8, 24} only, with a maximum cost of 0.018 (tex:968-974). Consistent in value but the body implies a continuous sweep.
- **Rendered vs unrendered draft.** OLS vs K-NN at HELM m = 1: 0.132 vs 0.137 (rendered, tex:989-990) and 0.132 vs 0.136 (draft, tex:2314). EEE m = 32: 0.061 vs 0.053 (rendered) and 0.066 vs 0.055 (draft). Indicates reruns between drafts.
- **Paper vs Space estimator.** Paper LRMC is rank 2, ridge 0.1, 5 inits (tex:811-813); Space whole-store LRMC is rank 8, ridge 0.03, 3 inits on top of a one-ability scale (space:pipeline/store.py:50-58). Paper embedder is gemini-embedding-001; Space uses EmbeddingGemma-2.
- **Paper vs Space completion margin on EEE.** Paper: ensemble 0.069 vs LRMC 0.096 on the dense 45 by 16 suite under uniform masking. Space, on the live store with 5-fold CV over pairs: ensemble 0.0577 vs LRMC 0.0587 (space:data/results.json). Different protocol, embedder and task mix, so not a direct contradiction, but the margin is about 27 times smaller in absolute terms.
- **Theorem estimator vs experimental estimator.** The guarded three-way-split estimator of Theorem 2 is not the one run in Section 4 (tex:1194-1219 vs tex:786-801).

## Missing Information

- No per-cell predictive uncertainty or calibration in the paper. How wide would a 90% interval be on the 0 to 1 scale?
- No experiment under non-uniform (selection-driven) missingness, although the paper names this as the main limitation (tex:662-668).
- No ablation over the embedding model, and no check that the result survives a cheaper or open embedder.
- How is the LRMC + PKPS blend weight "fit by held-out cross-validation against observed scores" structured (folds, unit of holdout)? (tex:823-824)
- Is LRMC's rank 2 appropriate at n = 10 or with 16 to 18 tasks? Rank was inherited from an 84 by 133 matrix and not re-selected, so the LRMC baseline may be under- or over-parameterised here.
- In completion, K-NN targets are residuals in standardised logit space. Standardisation and biases are estimated from observed sample scores at depth p_query; how sensitive is the comparison to noise in those sample scores (binomial noise at depth 3 to 32)? No measurement-error treatment is described.
- Are Wilcoxon p-values adjusted for the several comparisons reported? Tasks within a dataset (for example 5 MATH-MC levels) are not independent units.
- Theorem 1: what is kappa in the real suites, and is the bound non-vacuous at n = 45 or 93 and m <= 32?
- What fraction of EEE-store models have item-level response text at all? The Space reports 148 of 10,520 model identities with response coordinates.
- Does the transductive joint embedding matter? No out-of-sample (Nystrom) variant is evaluated.
