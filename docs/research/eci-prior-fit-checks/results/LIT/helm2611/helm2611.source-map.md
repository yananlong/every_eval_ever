## Canonical Sources

- For anything about the paper's method, data, protocol and numbers: `main.tex` lines 1 to 1370 wins.
- For what is deployed on EEE: the Space README and `store.py` win. The Space is a different estimator from the paper's (one-ability scale plus rank-8 ALS-WR plus PKPS plus anchor, a different embedder, different CV unit). Do not attribute Space numbers to the paper or vice versa.
- The unrendered draft after `\end{document}` loses to the rendered text wherever numbers differ.

## Coverage Gaps

- Figure contents were not rendered; only captions and the body's descriptions of the curves were read. Curve shapes (for example where the sample score overtakes PKPS on HELM) are as described by the authors.
- `pipeline/analyze.py`, `pkps.py`, `ensemble.py`, `sync.py`, `eee_ingest.py` were downloaded but only grepped, not read line by line.
- The predecessor paper (arXiv 2605.07096) was not read; its Theorem 2 is relied on by the transitivity remark.
- The released research embeddings (`evaleval/eee-pkps-embeddings` release 1.0.0) were not inspected.
- No code for the paper's own experiments was located in the tarball; the Space vendors a subset of `hhelm10/dkps`.
