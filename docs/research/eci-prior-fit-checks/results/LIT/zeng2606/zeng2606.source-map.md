## Canonical Sources

- The LaTeX source of v2 wins for all method, protocol and number questions.
- The dataset card wins for what is currently released. Its "paper matrix" is the August 2026 129 x 253 matrix; the paper's experiments use the May 2026 84 x 133 matrix.
- Where main text and appendix differ (Model-mean definition, provenance share), the appendix formula or the more specific statement is preferred and the conflict is logged in open questions.

## Coverage Gaps

- Figures were not rendered. The rank U-curves (Figure 4 and the block-holdout figure) are known only through captions and prose: the size of the gap between rank 1, 2 and 3, and whether rank-1 and rank-3 intervals overlap rank 2 in logit space, are not available from text.
- The GitHub repository `microsoft/benchpress` was not cloned; clip values, the exact fold construction and the sign handling of lower-is-better benchmarks could be resolved there.
- The dataset files themselves were not analysed here (a separate BENCHPRESS workstream covers the dataset vs Epoch and EEE; its REPORT.md did not exist when this pack was written).
- arXiv v1 was not compared with v2.
