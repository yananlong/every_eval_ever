# zeng2606 open questions

## Contradictions

- **"Within 4.6 points" (abstract) vs body.** The body number is a median absolute error of 4.63 (body:629). Half of held-out cells miss by more. The paper's own 90% conformal interval for the same predictor is 27.01 points wide and the 80% interval 19.56 (app:1407). The abstract does not say "median".
- **"A model's scores are largely determined by just two numbers" (abstract) vs the adopted model.** BenchPress uses a per-model offset plus a rank-2 interaction, that is three numbers per model, and the fitted matrix has rank up to 4 in transformed space (body:544-552). The two-factor statement matches Soft-Impute (app:604-610), which is 0.47 MedAE points worse than BenchPress (5.10 vs 4.63, app:769, app:752).
- **Rank selection is method-dependent.** Under validation, NMF selects r = 1 for all seven transforms and PMF selects r = 3 or 5 (app:787-830). The stable ranks of the complete submatrices are 1.11 to 1.28 and the first component alone explains 78 to 90% (body:396-399). "Rank 2" is the Soft-Impute optimum; the Bias ALS rank was never swept.
- **"Over 90% of the variation among models on the benchmarks they share" (abstract).** Computed on four fully observed submatrices of size 4 x 42, 7 x 11, 10 x 7 and 13 x 6 (body:396-399). After column centring a 13 x 6 matrix has at most 5 components and a 4 x 42 matrix at most 4, so two components explaining above 90% is weak evidence about a 133-benchmark matrix. Scale (raw vs logit) is not stated (body:408).
- **"Approximately rank 2 in logit space" as relayed in the task brief.** The abstract itself does not say "in logit space" for the rank claim; it says the matrix "is approximately rank-2" and that BenchPress is "a logit-space rank-2 matrix completion method" (body:13-15). The body shows the Soft-Impute minimum at rank 2 in both raw and logit space, with raw-space ranks 2 and 3 indistinguishable under block holdout (app:504-508).
- **Probe-set claim.** Abstract: five benchmarks "can recover the rest of a model's public scorecard to within 3.93 points" (body:16). Body: probe cells are counted "as exact predictions with zero error" and remain in the denominator, the probe set is selected on the same cells it is scored on, and the curves "should not be read as held-out transfer estimates" (body:743-745). So 3.93 is not an error on "the rest", and it is in-sample.
- **Temporal claim.** Finding: five seed scores bring predictions "within 4.83 points of the true value" (body:928). The metric includes revealed cells at zero error (body:919). Targets have 21 to 79 observed scores (app:1186-1212), so at k = 10 a 21-score model has 48% of its cells at zero error by construction (Grok 3 Beta: 0.5 at k = 10). The decline with k is partly mechanical.
- **Provenance share.** "Roughly half come from a source outside the model's own provider" (body:193) vs "Roughly four in five scores come from the model provider's own materials" (body:335) and "score provenance dominated by model-provider materials" (body:287).
- **Model-mean baseline definition.** Main text: "Adjust the benchmark mean by each model's overall strength percentile" (body:491-492). Appendix: x-hat_mb = x-bar_m. (app:545-549).
- **Holdout fraction.** "randomly hide half" (body:569) vs "three folds per model" (body:851, app:157) vs "about 20,000 test predictions per pair" (body:570), which is about 667 cells per fold, or 25.6% of 2,604, and matches neither one half (1,302) nor one third (868).
- **Paper matrix on the dataset card.** The card's "paper matrix" is 129 x 253 with 4,905 cells (15.0%), the August update, while the abstract and all main experiments use 84 x 133 with 2,604 cells (23.3%) (card; app:75).
- **Per-benchmark predictability denominator.** "35/49 of benchmarks fall below the 15% MedAPE threshold" (app:1296): only 49 of 133 columns are evaluated, with no stated inclusion rule. H7 uses 43 benchmarks (body:980).
- **PC1 sign claim vs lower-is-better columns.** "All 133 benchmarks load on it with the same sign" (body:420) while the matrix includes lower-is-better metrics (app:302-303, app:349-351) and no sign flip is described. Either a flip is applied silently or the completed matrix inherits the sign from the fit.
- **H1 and H4 (low-rank fit) not rejected** (body:996, body:1060): a benchmark's or model's rank-2 R^2 does not predict how well it is predicted, which sits awkwardly with the claim that rank-2 structure is what makes prediction work. The feature is computed on a zero-imputed matrix, which may explain it.

## Missing Information

- No two-way additive baseline is named as such. Model mean in standardised space (MedAE 7.22) is the closest; an ALS with R = 0 or R = 1 on the same pipeline would isolate what the second factor adds over a single factor.
- No rank sweep for Bias ALS; no uncertainty on Figure 4; numeric values of the rank curves are not in the text.
- No baseline for ranking preservation (what does model-mean or benchmark-mean completion achieve at margin 5?).
- Empirical coverage of the 90% conformal interval is not reported; only the 80% controlled variant reports coverage (app:1431-1434).
- No analysis of error by provenance (vendor-reported vs third-party) or by whether the cell matches the canonical setting.
- No treatment of binomial sampling noise: a 30-item benchmark has a standard error near 9 points at 50%, larger than the headline MedAE.
- No simulation or reweighting for selection-driven missingness; held-out cells are drawn from observed cells only.
- Clip constants for logit and probit are not given.
- For the HELM and EEE matrices: is rank 2 still optimal, and what are the fill rates' effects? Only MedAE and MedAPE against median baselines are reported (app:924-926). Implied fills are about 56.9% (HELM) and 7.7% (EEE).
- GPT-5.5 informed beats BenchPress (3.50 vs 4.63). The paper attributes this to memorised leaderboard facts but does not test it (for example on post-cutoff scores).
