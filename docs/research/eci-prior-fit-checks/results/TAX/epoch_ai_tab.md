Epoch AI
Epoch has three overlapping groupings. The most useful is the domain tag on each benchmark in the Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI). The ECI benchmark list can be filtered by twelve domains, and benchmarks often carry more than one tag. Each benchmark also records its source: Benchmark creator, Epoch AI or Model developer. These tags cover only the roughly 50 ECI benchmarks, not the 97 benchmarks tracked across the whole hub.
