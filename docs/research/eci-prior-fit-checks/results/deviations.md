# Deviations from the plan

Each entry: what changed, why, its effect. No registered threshold or rule was changed.

1. **Sampler for our models (M12, M14).** The plan says our models are sampled by our own NUTS call (1,000 tuning steps and 1,000 draws per chain, doubling to 4,000). On the owner's instruction F0 uses Epoch's own sampling routine: `fit_bayesian_eci` is called with `eci.bayesian.build_bayesian_model` replaced in memory by our builder (diagonal pilot, then dense mass matrix, Epoch's draw ladder and cold fallback). Expansions and PR use the same call with `_FREE_VARS` and `_DIAGNOSTIC_VARS` extended in memory for their added variables; F1's ladder is capped in memory at 4,000 draws per chain, as M14's fallback rule states. Effect: EQ compares the same sampler on both sides (and the F0 draws are bit-identical to Epoch's); expansion fits use the pilot and dense stages.
2. **Positions of misfit replicates (M4).** "As G-null" does not say which posterior draw a misfit replicate starts from. Replicate $r$ of a generator with $R_g$ replicates uses the pooled draw at position floor(r S / R_g) (R_g = 199 for G-null, 20 for each misfit generator), so the 20 replicates spread over all chains. Effect: none on the registered rules; the planted effects and seeds follow M4.
3. **Packages.** `h5netcdf` and `netCDF4` were installed in the environment because trace files could not be written without one (versions in `runs/env.json`). Epoch's `uv.lock` pins are otherwise unchanged.
4. **BFMI.** `az.bfmi` did not accept the DataTree in my sidecar helper, so the minimum BFMI in the W2 summary is taken from Epoch's own `sampler_summary` table (`epoch_sampler_summary.csv`); the sidecar entry reads `n/a`.
5. **Plan amendment R26 during W3.** After W3 started, the plan copy's `prior-grid.json` gained an exploratory key `jeffreys_prior_exploratory` (and the plan, claim map and run blocks were regenerated), on the owner's request. My freeze check compares `prior-grid.json` with that key removed: the digest then equals the one recorded at the freeze (`35c4f83e...`), so P1 to P10, X1, X2, PC, PR and the S5 rules are unchanged. The Jeffreys entry J is run by `w5j_fit.py` and is not part of the W5 script described here.
   Update: the key was renamed `jeffreys_priors_exploratory` (entries J1 and J2); the freeze check strips both names. After the pre-registered grid and PR, W5 runs `w5j_fit.py --variant J1`, `--variant J2` and `w5j_score.py` only if those scripts exist and smoke-test cleanly; they are exploratory and outside the C1 verdict.
9. **Amendment R30 (R28 and R29 belong to the anchor and Modrak agents): predictive checks before SBC, per prior arm.** On the user's decision, for each prior arm (Epoch default, PR, each grid entry, J2, J1) the order after the real-data fit exists is (1) T1-T7 on that fit with raw posterior predictive p-values, also ranked against the W3 Epoch null reference and labelled "approximate (reference calibrated under Epoch's prior)", valid only for the Epoch arm; then (2) posterior SBC (Modrak style) under that arm's prior. Nothing running in W3 is changed. No fits, even short ones, run while W3 runs. A per-arm fit-count and ETA goes to the coordinator before any SBC refit is launched.
   R30 also: every W5 arm (P0, P0b, P1-P10, X1, X2) saves a thinned .nc (500 draws per chain; posterior, per-draw ECI, log_likelihood) beside its npz, written by w5_sensitivity.py (no extra refits); .nc files stay out of git. J1 is run last among the W5 arms.
10. **Time cap per W3 replicate.** G-null replicate 40 spent more than 40 minutes in Epoch's draw ladder (pilot 886 s, first dense rung 933 s, still climbing) where a typical replicate takes about 3 minutes. The plan sets no limit for W3 fits (only F1 has a 60-minute cap), and an uncapped ladder (up to 16,000 dense draws, then 32,000 cold) could take many hours for one replicate. From replicate 40 on, each W3 replicate runs in its own process and is stopped after 60 minutes; a stopped replicate is recorded as non-converged (`timeout: true`), counted, and excluded from the null reference, SBC and power, as the plan does for any non-converged replicate. Replicates 0 to 39 finished before the cap and are unaffected (all converged at the first rung). Effect: the number of converged null replicates may fall slightly below 199; G1 needs at least 150.
11. **Outage.** The session worker restarted at about 23:07 on 2026-11-18, while G-null replicate 60 was in its 16,000-draw rung; no process ran until 07:16 on 2026-11-19 (about 8 hours lost). State on disk was intact: replicates 0 to 59 finished (replicate 40 recorded as a 60-minute timeout), replicate 60 had no sidecar. W3 was restarted from replicate 60 under the same 60-minute cap, with `setsid nohup`. Effect: schedule only; replicate 60 restarts from scratch with its deterministic seed.
