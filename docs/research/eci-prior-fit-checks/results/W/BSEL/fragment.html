15.5 Choice of benchmarks

Each refit deletes the rows of one or more benchmarks, re-applies Epoch's inclusion rule (at least four benchmarks and at least four scores between 0.1 and 0.9 per model) and refits with Epoch's function at P0's W5 settings. It is scored against P0 on the models present in both, with the S2 and S3 counts held at the registered 12, 1, 3 and 9. A4 covers the eight benchmarks on which both anchors have scores and is reported with no verdict. The other 49 leave-one-out refits, four residual clusters from the predictive checks, all 16 benchmarks of F at once and 20 random drops of 11 benchmarks each (rng 20261121) are exploratory. No drop removed an anchor. 80 of the 82 fits converged at seed 12345. The OTIS Mock AIME refit ended non-converged and the FrontierMath Tier 4 v1 refit hit the 2-hour cap, and both converged when rerun at seed 22345, which the table uses. A second seed of P0 moves no frontier model by more than 0.03 points.

Within A4, removing METR Time Horizons, SimpleBench, VPCT or WeirdML leaves every stratum and S4 passing, with frontier shifts of 0.06 to 0.88 points. Removing OTIS Mock AIME shrinks the raw capability gap between the anchors by 14%, so all 20 frontier models shift by more than 0.25 W, intervals widen by a median of 25% and the frontier stretch from rank 1 to rank 20 (13.32 in P0) grows by 2.51 points. GPQA diamond, MATH level 5 and DTBench fail S2 or S3 in at least the sparse stratum, with anchor-gap ratios of 0.91 to 1.03 over the other seven against a posterior coefficient of variation of 0.074 for the scale factor in P0. Shifts grow with distance below the anchors, and without OTIS Mock AIME Cerebras-GPT-13B falls by 18.4 points with all six of its own scores kept (Spearman correlation of shift with P0 ECI 0.97).

Across all 57 leave-one-out refits the top-10 set never changes and 47 pass S1 to S3 in every stratum. After OTIS Mock AIME, the largest frontier shifts follow removal of GPQA diamond (1.93 points), MATH level 5 (1.66), DTBench (1.61) and LMCA (1.32), and the stretch changes by −0.95 to +2.51. S4 fails 10 times on adjacent-pair probability changes of 0.10 to 0.30, each after removing a benchmark scored mostly by frontier models, such as APEX-Agents, both FrontierMath v2 tiers, FrontierSWE, ProofBench and Remote Labor Index. Of the clusters, the five older benchmarks remove 37 models and still pass everything, OTIS Mock AIME with LMCA stretches the frontier by 1.77 points and fails S2 and S3 in all four strata, and dropping all of F removes 103 models and stretches the frontier by 14.25 points.

Random 20% drops move the ECI more than most single removals. All 20 fail S2 in the all-model and dense strata, 18 fail the frontier on S1, S2 or S3, and 17 fail S4. The top-10 set changes in 4 of 20, each time Claude Fable 5 replacing Claude Opus 5 at rank 10. The frontier stretch changes by −1.74 to +4.24 points and the low-end mean by −14.8 to +15.4, with the slope of refit on P0 means ranging from 0.77 to 1.22. The median absolute shift is 4.5 points in the bottom quintile against 0.6 to 1.0 points elsewhere, and relative to interval width the tails move more than the models between the anchors. Under Epoch's model this suggests that the frontier order depends little on any one benchmark, while the gaps and the low end depend on which benchmarks are included about as much as on the W5 priors P8 and P9, which raised the low end by 14.0 and 17.2 points.
