15.x Anchors under the alternative priors

Each W5 arm and P0 were re-anchored on the same pair, with targets equal to P0's posterior-mean ECI of the two anchor models, and S1 to S4 were scored as in W5 with the anchors of both anchorings removed from S2 and S3. The pairs are the eight of block WA and two exploratory pairs with GPT-4o mini (P0 ECI 123.9) as the low anchor, against GPT-5 (X-lowGPT5) and against Claude Fable 5.2, the top model (X-lowTop). This is post-processing of the saved draws, it enters no verdict, and the C1 verdict stays on the original anchoring. Re-anchoring the stored ECI draws agrees with re-anchoring the raw capability to 1e-13 points, no draw has a non-positive map, and under the original pair the scores reproduce sensitivity.json for every arm (for P9 and P10 only from the first-pass arrays P9.npz and P10.npz, which are what W5 scored, while the retry3 draws used here give the same flags and counts).
